The work moved from the original cabinet's portrait/multiple-display arrangement to a single widescreen view, then refined the scope, aiming transforms, Story and Score Attack menus, transitions and presentation. Repeated playtests identified separate problems in hit detection, bullet-hole rendering and cabinet calibration. Those were corrected independently. Later iterations added paired artwork, the angled scope option and portable beta packaging.

These links document the development process. Current behavior, limitations and setup are described in the README; earlier experimental results are not current release guarantees.
